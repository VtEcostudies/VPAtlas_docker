VPAtlas Mobile App — Install Guide

Step-by-step instructions for installing the Vermont Vernal Pool Atlas on your phone

What You Need

  • Your phone (iPhone or Android)
  • The VPAtlas web address: https://vpatlas.org
  • iPhone users: You must use Safari (the browser that comes with your phone). Chrome, Firefox, and other browsers cannot add the app to your home screen on iPhone.

What the App Is For

The VPAtlas mobile app lets you:

  • Explore Vermont vernal pools on the map — no account needed.
  • Find pools near you in the field, using your phone's GPS.
  • Submit visit observations and surveys (requires a free VPAtlas account).
  • Work offline — the app remembers what you've looked at so you can use it without a signal.

iPhone Instructions

iPhone / iPad

Step 1 — Open the site in Safari

  1. Open Safari on your phone.
  2. Type or tap https://vpatlas.org in the address bar.
  3. The VPAtlas Explore map will load. You can browse around now if you want.

Step 2 — Add the app to your Home Screen

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  2. Scroll down in the share menu and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. If you see a setting called "Open as Web App", make sure it is turned on.
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

Step 3 — Open the app

  1. Find the new VPAtlas icon on your home screen and tap it.
  2. The app opens directly to the Explore map.

Step 4 — Sign in to submit data (optional)

You can browse pools without signing in. To submit visits or surveys, you'll need a free VPAtlas account:

  1. Tap the menu button (three lines, top-left of the screen).
  2. Tap Login.
  3. Enter your username and password, or tap Register to create a new account.
Sign in inside the app, not in Safari. On iPhone, the installed app keeps its data separate from Safari. If you signed in earlier in Safari, you'll need to sign in again inside the installed app the first time.

Step 5 — Close the browser

Switch back to Safari and close the vpatlas.org tab you used during setup. From now on, always open VPAtlas using the icon on your home screen — that's the installed app, with offline support.

Android Instructions

Android

Step 1 — Open the site in Chrome

  1. Open Chrome on your phone.
  2. Type or tap https://vpatlas.org in the address bar.
  3. The VPAtlas Explore map will load.

Step 2 — Install the app

  1. Tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen. (The wording depends on your version of Chrome.)
  3. Tap Install (or Add) to confirm.

Step 3 — Open the app

  1. Find the new VPAtlas icon on your home screen.
  2. Tap it to open the app.

Step 4 — Sign in to submit data (optional)

If you were already signed in in Chrome, the installed app usually carries over the sign-in. To sign in or out:

  1. Tap the menu button (three lines, top-left).
  2. Tap Login.
  3. Enter your username and password, or tap Register to create a new account.

Step 5 — Close the browser

Close the Chrome tab you used during setup. From now on, open VPAtlas from the home screen icon.

If you accidentally remove the icon from your home screen: The app is not deleted. Swipe up to see all your installed apps, find VPAtlas, and drag it back to your home screen — or reinstall it using these steps.

Common Questions

Can I use VPAtlas without installing it?
Yes. Just visit vpatlas.org in any browser. Installing as an app is recommended for fieldwork because it works offline, opens faster, and avoids using mobile data each time you launch it.
I can't find "Add to Home Screen" on iPhone.
You must be using Safari. Chrome, Firefox, and other browsers on iPhone do not show "Add to Home Screen". Open the site in Safari and try again.
I installed the app but can't sign in.
The installed app's storage is separate from your browser's, especially on iPhone. Open the app from the home screen icon, tap the menu (top-left), and tap Login.
The app says it can't connect.
That's normal in areas without signal. Once you've used the app while online, it caches what you've seen so you can keep using it offline. When you get back to an area with service, it will update automatically.
Do I need an account to use VPAtlas?
No — browsing pools on the map is free and open to everyone. You only need an account to submit visits, surveys, or new pool records. Accounts are free; tap Register in the menu.
I see an old version of VPAtlas.
VPAtlas was rebuilt in May 2026. If your browser is still showing the old design, it's serving a cached copy. Tap and hold the home-screen icon → remove the app, then reinstall using the steps above. (Or clear your browser's site data for vpatlas.org.)
How do I update the app?
You don't need to. The app updates itself whenever you open it while connected to the internet. New features and bug fixes arrive automatically.
