Using the Top Filters

Control which vernal pools appear on the Explore screen

The bar across the top of the Explore (home) screen filters which pools you see. The pool list, the map, and the summary panel all update together as you change filters. Your choices are remembered between sessions.

Data-type buttons

  • All — every pool in the Atlas.
  • Mine — pools connected to your account (shown when you're signed in).
  • Review — pools awaiting review (administrators only).

Pool ID search

The Pool… box matches part of a pool ID as you type and shows suggestions. Pick one to jump straight to that pool.

Town and County

The Town… and County… boxes let you type to search and pick one or more. Each choice appears as a removable chip — tap its × to clear it.

Indicator Spp

Tick Indicator Spp to show only pools whose visits recorded an indicator species.

Near me

The Near me stepper (with − and + buttons, e.g. “5 km”) limits the list to pools within that distance of your location. It needs GPS — see Enabling GPS & Compass.

Status chips

A row of colored chips: Potential, Probable, Confirmed (plus Duplicate and Eliminated for administrators). A chip is colored when its status is shown and grey when hidden. Tap a chip to toggle it.

Survey-level chips

  • Mapped (circle) — pool has been mapped but not yet visited.
  • Visited (triangle) — pool has at least one Atlas visit.
  • Monitored (diamond) — pool has a monitoring survey.
  • Reviewed (checkmark, administrators only) — pool has a completed review.

These are “show any of these” toggles — turning a chip on widens what appears. Turn one off to hide that level.

Filters stack. “Confirmed” status + “Stowe” town + “Visited” level shows only confirmed, visited pools in Stowe. To start fresh, clear the town and county chips and turn all the status and level chips back on.
