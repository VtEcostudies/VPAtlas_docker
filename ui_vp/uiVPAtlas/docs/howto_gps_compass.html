Enabling GPS & Compass

Let the app see where you are and which way you're facing

GPS shows where you are; the compass shows which way you're facing. Together they let the Pool Finder point you to a pool. Your phone asks permission before sharing either — here's how to grant it.

Enabling GPS

On the Explore map (“Near me”)

  1. In the top filter bar, use the Near me stepper.
  2. Your phone asks to use your location — tap Allow.
  3. Pools within the chosen radius are shown.

In the Pool Finder

  1. Open Find Pool for any pool.
  2. Tap the GPS button — a crosshairs-and-person icon labeled Zoom to my GPS location.
  3. Tap Allow when your phone asks.
  4. A green dot in the header means GPS is tracking, and the map centers on you.
On iPhone Choose Allow While Using App. You may be asked again each time you open the app — that's normal iPhone behavior.

Enabling the Compass

The compass is the round dial in the bottom-right corner of the Pool Finder map.

  • Android: the compass usually starts working on its own.
  • iPhone: the compass shows a card that says Tap this dialog to enable compass orientation. Tap it, then approve the motion & orientation request.

Once enabled, the arrow rotates to point toward your selected pool as you turn.

iPhone forgets compass permission. iPhone does not remember compass permission between sessions. Each time you open the Pool Finder you may need to tap the card again to re-enable the compass.

If something isn't working

  • No location: check that Location Services are on in your phone's settings, and that VPAtlas (or your browser) is allowed to use it.
  • Compass arrow stuck: leave the Pool Finder and reopen it, then tap the compass card again. Calibrate the phone by moving it in a figure-8.
  • Both GPS and compass work best outdoors with a clear view of the sky.
