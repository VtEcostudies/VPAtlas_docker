Updating the App Before Going Out

Make sure you have the latest app and pool data before you lose signal

VPAtlas works offline in the field — but only with whatever it downloaded the last time it had a connection. Before a field trip, take a minute on wifi or cell data to bring both the app and the pool data up to date.

Sign in while you have a good connection — before you head out. Signing in requires a network. If you're already in the field with poor or no service and you aren't signed in, you won't be able to create or submit a visit, survey, or new pool. Sign in at home or anywhere with solid wifi or cell service; the app keeps you signed in afterwards.

The app updates itself

  • VPAtlas is an installed web app. Each time you open it with an internet connection, it automatically checks for a new version.
  • If an update is found, the app downloads and installs it on the spot — you'll briefly see a dark Downloading update… / Activating update… screen — then it reloads itself onto the new version. You don't have to close or reopen anything.
  • Your current version is shown in the top bar, just under the VPAtlas name (for example, v3.5.305).
  • If the version number doesn't change after the brief Activating screen — or you see a yellow "An update is ready, but auto-reload was paused" message at the bottom — open the menu (☰) → Profile and tap Reset App to force the update through. This is uncommon and just means the app recently reloaded for another reason; the safety check waits a minute before reloading again.

Before you head out

  1. While you still have wifi or a cell signal, open VPAtlas from your home-screen icon.
  2. If an update is available, let the brief Downloading update… / Activating update… screen finish — the app reloads itself when it's done. If no such screen appears, you're already on the latest version.
  3. Make sure you're signed in. Tap the menu (☰) → Login and enter your username and password. Login requires a network, so do it now while signal is good — you can't sign in offline.
  4. Check the version number under VPAtlas in the top bar. The newest version is listed at the top of the most recent changelog, here in Documentation.

Refresh the pool data

The map and pool list are stored on your device separately from the app itself, and they do not refresh on their own. Pull the latest records before you go:

  1. Tap the menu button (☰, three lines) in the top-right corner.
  2. Tap Refresh Pool Data.
  3. Wait for the list to reload. You now have the latest pool records saved for offline use.
Offline is handled gracefully. If you tap Refresh Pool Data with no connection, the app simply tells you it needs a network and leaves your existing data in place. It will not show an error or lose anything.
Do this where the signal is good. Update the app and refresh the pool data at home or anywhere with solid wifi. In the field, the app runs entirely from what it has already saved.
