Caching Basemaps for Offline Use

Pre-load the map background so it works where there's no signal

The map background — aerial imagery and topographic tiles — is saved automatically as you look at it. There is no “download” button: the app simply keeps every map tile you bring on-screen. To have a usable map offline, view your field area while you still have a connection.

How map caching works

  • Every time you pan or zoom the map, the tiles you see are saved onto your device.
  • Saved tiles stay available offline, and they are kept even when the app updates.
  • Tiles you never viewed are not saved. If you never zoomed into an area, it will be blank offline.

Before you head out

  1. While on wifi or a cell signal, open VPAtlas and go to the map.
  2. Navigate to the area where you'll be working.
  3. Pan slowly across the whole area so every part of it appears on-screen at least once.
  4. Zoom in to the closest zoom level you expect to use in the field, and pan across the area again at that zoom. Then zoom out a step or two and pan once more.
  5. If you use more than one map background, switch to each one and repeat — each background is its own separate set of tiles.
Think of it as showing the app where you're going. Whatever you looked at, you'll have offline. Whatever you skipped over, you won't.
Map tiles can be large. Pre-load on wifi when you can, so you don't use a lot of cell data covering a wide area at high zoom.

In the field

The areas you viewed display normally with no connection. If you reach a spot you didn't pre-load, that part of the map will be blank — but pool markers, the pool list, and data entry all keep working regardless.
