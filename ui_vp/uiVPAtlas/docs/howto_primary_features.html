Find Pool, Atlas Visit & New Pool

The three actions that cover most field work in VPAtlas

Three actions cover most field work: navigating to a pool, recording a visit to a known pool, and adding a pool that isn't in the Atlas yet. Submitting data requires a free account — sign in from the menu first.

Find Pool

Find Pool opens the Pool Finder, which uses your GPS and a pointing compass arrow to walk you to a pool.

  • From a pool's detail page or its map popup, tap Find Pool.
  • Or: tap a pool in the list to select it, then open the menu (☰) — a Find Pool <pool ID> shortcut appears at the top.

The Pool Finder shows your distance to the pool and an arrow pointing toward it. See Enabling GPS & Compass to set those up.

+ Atlas Visit

An Atlas Visit records an observation at a pool that is already in the Atlas.

  • Open a pool's detail page or map popup and tap + Atlas Visit.
  • The visit form opens already linked to that pool. Work through the pages and submit.

+ New Pool

Use New Pool when you find a vernal pool that isn't in the Atlas yet.

  • On the Explore screen with no pool selected, the summary panel on the right shows a + New Pool button (visible when you're signed in). Tap it to open a blank visit form that creates the new pool.
  • You can also press and hold a spot on the map to start a new pool at that location.
Check before you add. Not sure whether a pool is already mapped? Use Pool ID search or zoom in on the map first. Only add a New Pool when you're confident it isn't already there.
You must be signed in to submit. A free account is required to submit a visit, survey, or new pool. Browsing the map needs no account — sign in from the menu (☰) when you're ready to contribute.
