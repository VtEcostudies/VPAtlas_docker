Using the Pool Finder

Navigate to a vernal pool in the field with GPS and a pointing compass

The Pool Finder walks you to a pool using your phone's GPS and a compass arrow. Optionally, record a GPS track of where you walked. Browsing works without an account; submitting a visit, survey, or track requires signing in from the menu (☰).

Opening the Pool Finder

  • From a pool's detail page or its map popup, tap Find Pool.
  • Or: select a pool in the Explore list, open the menu (☰), and tap the Find Pool <pool ID> shortcut at the top.

Selected pools appear as chips across the top of the screen. You can have more than one in play at once.

Your location and Position Accuracy

Your position shows on the map as a blue dot inside a shaded accuracy circle — the bigger the circle, the less certain the fix. The pill on the map reads Position Accuracy (5m), where the number is the current GPS uncertainty in metres and the coloured dot grades it (green = good, amber/red = poor).

Wait for the number to settle. GPS often opens at 30–50 m and tightens over the first minute or two outdoors. A reading under ~10 m is solid for pool navigation. If it stays high, step into the open away from tree cover and buildings.

If GPS or compass aren't working, see Enabling GPS & Compass.

The compass arrow

The compass dial points toward the nearest selected pool. Tap it once to enable the device-orientation compass so the arrow turns as you turn. Walk in the direction the arrow points; the distance updates as you close in.

Framing the map

The zoom buttons on the map jump the view to what you need:

  • Zoom to pools — frames the selected pool(s).
  • Zoom to my location — centres on your GPS dot.
  • Zoom to both — fits you and the pool(s) in one view, so you can see how far you have to go.
  • Zoom to track — frames the track you're recording (appears only while a track is active).

Recording a track

A track is a breadcrumb trail of where you walked — useful for documenting an access route to a pool.

  1. Open the menu (☰) and tap Start Track.
  2. A red dashed line draws behind you as you move. The Track Points pill on the map shows the live point count, e.g. Track Points (26).
  3. When you're done, open the menu again and tap Stop Track. A dialog gives you three choices:
    • Cancel — keep recording, close the dialog.
    • Discard — throw the points away; nothing is saved.
    • Save — save the track on your phone.

Saved tracks wait on your device until you upload them. Review and upload them from My Visits and Tracks (also reachable via View Track in the menu). Uploaded tracks are stored in your VPAtlas account and only visible to you.

Tracks survive a reload. If the page reloads while a track is active, recording resumes automatically — you won't lose the points collected so far.

Starting a Visit

Once you've reached a pool, launch the data forms straight from its entry in the Pool Finder: + Atlas Visit records the data from your visit to each pool. See Find Pool, Atlas Visit & New Pool for how those forms work.

Cache the map before you lose signal. The Pool Finder needs map tiles to be useful in the field. Load the area you're headed to while you still have a connection — see Caching Basemaps for Offline Use.
